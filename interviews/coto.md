ABOUT THE COMPANY

coto is an emotional wellness platform at the intersection of human expertise and AI guidance. Our engineering team delivers across mobile (iOS and Android), backend services, and AI systems that must remain in tight operational sync. We are a small, fast-moving team where delivery discipline has a direct and measurable impact on outcomes.

POSITION OVERVIEW

The Engineering Delivery Lead is responsible for the end-to-end health of the delivery function — from sprint planning and acceptance criteria ownership through to release coordination and post-sprint closure. This is not a software engineering role; however, the ideal candidate possesses a strong engineering foundation and is comfortable working at the intersection of product, engineering, and quality assurance.

The role reports directly to the Head of Engineering and coordinates a cross-functional team comprising four mobile engineers, three backend engineers, a DevOps engineer, two product managers, one designer, and two QA engineers.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

- Maintain Jira as the authoritative source of truth for all work in progress; conduct regular reconciliation of board state against the git repository and surface discrepancies proactively.

- Translate product requirements documents (PRDs) into well-structured, testable acceptance criteria; ensure that acceptance criteria are visible at the ticket level, referenced in pull requests, and understood by all contributors prior to development commencing.

- Facilitate daily standups, asynchronous check-ins, and sprint review ceremonies in a manner that drives tangible progress rather than status reporting.

- Monitor cycle time, sprint carry-over, and release readiness across mobile, backend, and DevOps; identify blockers early and escalate with appropriate context and urgency.

- Coordinate cross-platform release sequencing, dependency mapping, and rollback planning across iOS, Android, and backend services.

- Communicate scope changes transparently to product and engineering leadership, including impact assessment and revised timelines.

- Develop and maintain lightweight agent-assisted workflows — using tools such as Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor — to automate delivery digests, PRD summarisation, and Jira–git reconciliation reporting.

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS & EXPERIENCE

- 2–5 years of total professional experience, including a minimum of 1–2 years in a hands-on software engineering capacity (backend, frontend, or mobile). Candidates who have transitioned from an individual contributor engineering role into delivery, programme management, or technical project management are strongly preferred.

- Demonstrable experience owning and administering a project management tool (Jira preferred; Linear or ClickUp considered) for an engineering team — including workflow configuration, sprint management, and reporting.

- Proven ability to author clear, unambiguous acceptance criteria from product requirement inputs and to enforce adherence throughout the delivery lifecycle.

- Proficiency with git fundamentals — branching, commit history, pull request review — sufficient to reconcile repository state against project board status.

- Strong written communication in English, with the ability to produce concise status updates, escalation summaries, and decision logs.

- Demonstrated closing discipline: a track record of driving work to completion, with measurable improvement in carry-over or cycle time metrics.

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

- Experience applying large language models (LLMs) to document synthesis, specification summarisation, or delivery reporting — not primarily for code generation.

- Familiarity with engineering flow metrics, including cycle time, lead time for changes, and deployment frequency (DORA framework).

- Exposure to CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, or equivalent) and production monitoring tooling (Datadog, Grafana, or equivalent).

- Working knowledge of Node.js, TypeScript, or MongoDB — sufficient to interrogate pull requests, not to contribute production code.

- Prior experience in consumer technology, wellness, or healthcare platforms.
